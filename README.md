## 📺 Favorite Series: *One Piece*

> "I'm gonna be King of the Pirates!" — Monkey D. Luffy

### About
**One Piece** is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by **Eiichiro Oda**. It started in 1997 and later became a hugely popular anime. It follows **Monkey D. Luffy**, a boy whose body gained rubber powers after eating a Devil Fruit. He sails the Grand Line with his crew, the **Straw Hat Pirates**, in search of the legendary treasure **One Piece** to become the Pirate King.

### Main Straw Hat Crew
1. Monkey D. Luffy – Captain
2. Roronoa Zoro – Swordsman
3. Nami – Navigator
4. Usopp – Sniper
5. Sanji – Cook
6. Tony Tony Chopper – Doctor
7. Nico Robin – Archaeologist
8. Franky – Shipwright
9. Brook – Musician
10. Jinbe – Helmsman

### Why I Love It
- ⚓ Amazing adventure and world-building
- 🤝 Strong themes of **friendship**, **freedom**, and **dreams**
- 😭 Emotional moments that hit hard
- 😂 Funny characters and unforgettable arcs

## 🎬 Favorite Movie: *A Silent Voice* (聲の形)

### About
**A Silent Voice** (*Koe no Katachi*) is a 2016 Japanese animated drama film by **Kyoto Animation**, directed by **Naoko Yamada**. It is based on the manga by **Yoshitoki Ōima**. The story follows **Shoya Ishida**, a former bully who tries to make amends with **Shoko Nishimiya**, a Deaf girl he tormented in elementary school.

### Themes
- *Forgiveness*
- *Redemption*
- *Communication*
- *Mental health and self-worth*

### Why I Love It
- [x] Beautiful animation
- [x] Touching and realistic story
- [x] Powerful message about kindness and second chances
- [x] Great soundtrack
